Ledger

A page from a careful lab notebook: a numbered family, a serif specimen name, a ruled data field that carries the dose, the form, the servings and the net quantity. One brand colour under two percent of the panel, one accent per product, no gloss, no arcs, no claims.

Family lineup

Five products in catalogue order. Powders first (morning products, matte bone tubs with a same-colour cap), then capsules and softgels in amber with a black cap. The accent disc on the cap is what you see from above on a counter.

Containers are illustrative, drawn at true relative scale (3 px per mm). Caps and label widths follow the suggested containers listed under each product; final sizes come from the supplier dielines.

Front panels, flat

The readable chord of each wrap label at 4 px per mm. The ruled field and the hairlines continue around the container; the rest of the wrap holds Supplement Facts, directions, allergens, lot and best-by, distributor, UPC and the QR to the certificate of analysis.

Caps from above

A 30 mm (tubs) or 20 mm (bottles) accent disc with the catalogue numeral, applied after capping. Colour is never the only cue: the number is always beside it.

Grid (H = label height)

  1. One left-aligned column. Margin 0.08 H on tubs, 0.06 H on bottles. Nothing centred.
  2. Logo lockup top-left, 0.36 H wide. Helix in teal, everything else in Ink.
  3. Name block anchored from the bottom: name baseline 0.50 H, descriptor baseline 0.58 H, a two-line name grows upward. The gap between logo and name stays empty.
  4. "Dietary Supplement" in bold at 0.62 H completes the statement of identity (21 CFR 101.3).
  5. Ledger field 0.66 to 0.86 H: 0.3 pt hairlines every 0.04 H, full wrap width. Colour chip 0.08 H square at the margin; "No. NN" beside it; three rows on the rules: dose and timing, form, servings.
  6. Net quantity alone in the bottom band, baseline 0.95 H, inside the lower 30 percent of the principal display panel; numerals 3.2 mm, or 4.8 mm where the panel exceeds 25 sq in (21 CFR 101.105).
  7. Catalogue numeral blind-debossed upper right, cap 0.16 H, baseline 0.36 H on tubs and 0.40 H on bottles.
  8. Accent in three places only: chip, cap disc, spine stripe on the back seam.

Materials

  1. Textured wet-strength label paper (Tintoretto Gesso, Raflatac Gesso or Fasson Rustique class) with a matte water-resistant varnish.
  2. Rich-black ink build; teal as a spot where offered. HP Indigo for runs under 500 per SKU.
  3. One finish: the blind deboss. No foil, no spot UV, no soft-touch laminate.
  4. Tubs matte bone HDPE with same-colour cap; bottles amber glass or amber PET with a matte black cap. No silver caps.
