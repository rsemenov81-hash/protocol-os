Questions

Can I buy these now?

Not yet. The store is in private preview while labels, prices and shipping are settled. The product pages show what we know today.

How long does one container last?

It depends on the product. At one serving a day: creatine 50 days, vanilla whey isolate 24, chocolate whey blend 15, collagen peptides 14, magnesium glycinate 30, vitamin D3 100. Check the serving count before you plan around a jar.

Is the collagen a complete protein?

No. A 20 g scoop has 18 g of protein, but collagen is missing some essential amino acids. It doesn’t replace whey or the protein in your meals.

What is the difference between the two whey products?

The vanilla is whey isolate. The chocolate is a blend of isolate and concentrate. Both list 22 g of protein in a two-scoop serving, and both contain milk.

How much magnesium is in a serving?

Three capsules give you 275 mg of magnesium. The label also says 2,500 mg of magnesium glycinate. That is the weight of the whole compound, so the two numbers measure different things.

Is the vitamin D3 vegan or soy-free?

No. The softgel is gelatin, and the fill includes soybean oil and corn oil. There is no vitamin K2 in it.

Who should check with a clinician first?

Anyone who is pregnant or nursing, under 18, on medication or managing a medical condition. Supplements can interact with medicines. Follow the directions on the label and don’t exceed the stated serving.
