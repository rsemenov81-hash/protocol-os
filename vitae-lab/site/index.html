Questions

Can I buy these now?

Soon. The store is in private preview while labels, prices and shipping are settled.

How long does one container last?

At one serving a day: creatine 50 days, vanilla whey isolate 24, chocolate whey blend 15, collagen peptides 14, magnesium glycinate 30, vitamin D3 100.

What is the difference between the two whey products?

The vanilla is whey isolate. The chocolate is a blend of isolate and concentrate. Both give you 22 g of protein in two scoops, and both contain milk.

Is the collagen a complete protein?

No. A 20 g scoop has 18 g of protein, but collagen is missing some essential amino acids, so it works alongside whey or the protein in your meals, not in place of them.

How much magnesium is in a serving?

Three capsules give you 275 mg of magnesium. The label also lists 2,500 mg of magnesium glycinate, which is the weight of the whole compound.

Is the vitamin D3 vegan or soy-free?

No. The softgel is gelatin, and the fill includes soybean oil and corn oil. It has no vitamin K2.

Who should check with a clinician first?

Anyone who is pregnant or nursing, under 18, on medication or managing a medical condition. Follow the directions on the label and keep to the stated serving.
