Questions

Can I buy these now?

Not yet. The store is in private preview while labels, prices and shipping are finalized. Product pages show the facts we have today.

How long does one container last?

It depends on the product. At one serving a day: creatine 50 days, vanilla whey isolate 24, chocolate whey blend 15, collagen peptides 14, magnesium glycinate 30, vitamin D3 100. Check the serving count before you plan around a jar.

Is the collagen a complete protein?

No. A 20 g scoop provides 18 g of protein, but collagen lacks the full set of essential amino acids. It does not replace whey or the protein in meals.

What is the difference between the two whey products?

The vanilla is whey protein isolate. The chocolate is a blend of whey isolate and whey concentrate. Both list 22 g of protein per two-scoop serving. Both contain milk.

How much magnesium is in a serving?

Three capsules provide 275 mg of elemental magnesium. That comes from 2,500 mg of magnesium glycinate, which is the weight of the whole compound. The two numbers measure different things.

Is the vitamin D3 vegan or soy-free?

No. The softgel is made with gelatin, and the fill includes soybean oil and corn oil. It contains no vitamin K2.

Who should check with a clinician first?

Anyone who is pregnant or nursing, under 18, taking medication or managing a medical condition. Supplements can interact with medicines. Follow the directions on the label and do not exceed the stated serving.
